01 Featured projects

OsudSheba

AI medication adherence platform

A bilingual (Bangla/English) app that helps patients take medicines on time and lets caregivers follow along. Users photograph a prescription; the app reads it and turns it into a dose schedule with voice reminders.

  • ~16.7K lines of Dart across 11 feature modules with Riverpod, GoRouter route guards and Hive offline storage.
  • Prescription scanning: on-device ML Kit OCR plus an Express endpoint calling Gemini 2.5 Flash that returns structured medicine/dose JSON for review.
  • Backend secured with Firebase ID-token and subscription middleware, rate limiting and HMAC-SHA256-verified carrier-billing webhooks.
  • Bangla text-to-speech dose alerts, adherence charts, caregiver dashboard, and a Next.js/TypeScript companion site with OTP subscription.
